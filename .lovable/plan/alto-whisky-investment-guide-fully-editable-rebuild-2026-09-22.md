# Alto Whisky Investment Guide — Fully Editable Rebuild

## Goal
Rebuild the complete 15-page A4 guide so its wording is no longer baked into page images. Preserve the existing visual composition and text placement while making the text independently editable in Canva-compatible software.

## Deliverables
- **Editable PowerPoint master (`.pptx`)** — the primary Canva import file, with separate text boxes, images, shapes, logos, page furniture, and page numbers.
- **Selectable-text PDF (`.pdf`)** — exported from the rebuilt master for review and distribution.
- Both files will retain the existing portrait A4 proportions and page order.

## Rebuild approach
1. **Audit all 15 pages**
   - Extract and proofread every heading, paragraph, caption, quotation, statistic, label, footer, and page number.
   - Record each element’s position, alignment, font treatment, colour, and approximate size from the supplied source PDF.

2. **Separate artwork from text**
   - Reconstruct clean photographic and decorative backgrounds without the baked-in wording.
   - Keep photographs as image elements and recreate panels, borders, lines, icons, badges, and page furniture as separate editable objects where practical.
   - Preserve the award badges, implied distillery relationship, existing return figure, and Scotch Whisky Association quotations.

3. **Recreate all typography**
   - Match the current guide’s **EB Garamond and Noto Sans** appearance, weights, sizing, line spacing, alignment, and hierarchy.
   - Place every passage in an independent editable text box at the same visual location as the reference.
   - Apply the previously agreed spelling, grammar, clarity, repetition, contact-detail, and short-risk-line corrections without changing the intended message.

4. **Standardise page furniture**
   - Correct and align page numbers consistently at the top left and bottom right.
   - Replace the final page’s bottom-right number with the official Alto logo.
   - Retain the agreed contact route and concise risk wording on the final page.

5. **Prepare for Canva editing**
   - Keep text boxes, images, logos, and shapes ungrouped or logically grouped so imported pages remain practical to edit.
   - Embed all media in the PowerPoint and use broadly compatible font files.
   - Avoid flattening complete pages into single images.

6. **Quality assurance and review**
   - Validate the PowerPoint structure and confirm every page contains real editable text.
   - Extract the finished text to check completeness, spelling, page order, and absence of placeholders.
   - Render all 15 pages from both outputs and visually compare them against the source for placement, clipping, wrapping, alignment, image quality, numbering, and logo treatment.
   - Complete at least one correction-and-recheck cycle before delivering the draft.

## Important compatibility note
PowerPoint will be the editable master because Canva generally preserves its separate text and design elements more reliably than importing a PDF. Canva may substitute a font if the exact font is unavailable in the user’s Canva account; the supplied PDF will show the intended final appearance for comparison.
