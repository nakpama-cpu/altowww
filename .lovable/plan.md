# Alto Whisky Investment Guide — High-Resolution Review Draft

## Outcome
Create a polished 15-page A4 review draft that preserves the current luxury, photography-led character while making the guide sharper, cleaner and visually consistent with the Alto Whisky website.

## What will stay unchanged
- Keep the award badges and the implied distillery relationships.
- Keep the existing 10–15% return wording for this draft.
- Keep the quotations attributed to the Scotch Whisky Association.
- Preserve the existing page subjects, photography style and overall navy, copper and warm-white character.
- Use only the agreed short risk line rather than the full website disclaimer: “Capital at risk. Values can rise or fall. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.”

## Draft work
1. Rebuild the pages at print-ready A4 quality rather than enlarging the existing 90 PPI page artwork.
2. Recreate headings, body copy, rules, icons, labels and page furniture as crisp elements using Cormorant Garamond and Inter.
3. Retain and enhance the current imagery, improving sharpness and tonal consistency without changing its look and feel.
4. Apply a consistent layout system: margins, columns, heading scale, copper dividers, icon sizing, captions and footer spacing.
5. Correct clear errors such as “MATURETY,” awkward image lettering and inconsistent labels, while preserving the approved claims and quotations.
6. Tighten repetitive wording across the managed service, investment journey, ownership and portfolio pages without removing the underlying messages.
7. Improve crowded pages through better hierarchy, spacing and concise copy, particularly the market, portfolio and FAQ pages.
8. Standardise guide numbering: cover and contents remain unnumbered; content pages run 1–13; each numbered page uses the same top-left and bottom-right font, size and fixed position.
9. On the final page, retain the top-left page number and replace the bottom-right number with the Alto Whisky logo.
10. Add Alto Whisky’s public Google Business Profile contact details to the final page after confirming the exact live listing, alongside the website address and consultation call to action.
11. Add the agreed short risk line discreetly on the final page.
12. Export a compressed, high-resolution review PDF with embedded fonts, selectable text and document metadata.

## Review and quality checks
- Render and visually inspect every page for clipping, overlaps, spacing, legibility and image quality.
- Check page-number alignment and footer consistency across the full document.
- Confirm the final-page logo and contact block are balanced and readable.
- Check all retained figures, badges and quotations for consistent presentation; this draft will not independently amend the user-approved claims.
- Deliver the draft PDF for review before preparing any final production version.
